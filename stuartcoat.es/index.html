Stuart Coates

I run hospitality operations in Portland, Oregon.

Ten years across boutique hotels, a wellness club, and a lot of events. Most of my work is the part guests never see: the SOPs, the training manual, the booking-system migration, the front desk rebuilt from a crew of five. The goal is a property that runs the same on a short-staffed Tuesday as it did on opening night.

    119 rooms · U.S. West Coast launch
